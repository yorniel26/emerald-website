Good questions

Do you charge for estimates?

No. We look at the job and give you a written price before any work starts. You decide, no pressure.

Is there a minimum job size?

Small jobs are welcome. Grouping a few small repairs in one visit saves you money.

Do you come to my RV?

Yes. Tell us the make, model and year when you book, and where it’s parked: home, storage lot or campground.

How long does an epoxy garage floor take?

Most garages take a couple of days, including prep and cure time. You get the exact timeline with your estimate.

Do you work on rentals and vacation homes?

Yes. Owners and property managers can book for a property they aren't at. Just tell us how we get in.

Can you help after a storm?

Yes. Pick "Urgent" when you book and send photos. Downed fence panels and leaks go first.

How do I pay?

You pay when the job is done and you're happy with it. We take card, cash and the usual payment apps.
